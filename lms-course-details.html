About This Course

Build Confidence in Your Responsibilities

This training is designed to help supervisors understand the responsibilities associated with reasonable suspicion testing. The course focuses on recognizing observable indicators, documenting concerns, and following appropriate workplace procedures.

Complete the course at your own pace and return whenever you need to continue your training.

Learning Outcomes

What You Will Learn

Recognize observable signs and indicators that may require further action.
Understand the supervisor's role in the reasonable suspicion process.
Identify appropriate documentation and communication practices.
Apply course concepts to common workplace situations.
